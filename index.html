About Me

I am a PhD student in the Kahlert School of Computing at the University of Utah, advised by Prof. Aditya Bhaskara. I develop algorithms for graph optimization, robust network design, and machine learning, using probability, linear algebra, and convex optimization to establish provable guarantees.

Prior to this, I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at IIT Bhubaneswar in 2019 and then worked at Deloitte USI for two years, from 2019 to 2021.

Research

Structure-Preserving Feature Selection and Graph Augmentation

I develop algorithms to select informative, representative features from high-dimensional data under multiple matrix norms. This work connects data summarization and dimensionality reduction with graph augmentation and effective resistance.

Paper: Graph Augmentation for Min Effective Resistances using Column Subset Selection

Probabilistic Connectivity and Network Robustness

I study how adding edges can improve worst-case connectivity in probabilistic networks. Using tools from percolation theory, I analyze networks under stochastic failures and design algorithms with connectivity guarantees.

Paper: Optimizing Probabilistic Propagation in Graphs by Adding Edges

Effective Resistance and Network Efficiency

I develop clustering-based edge-augmentation algorithms to minimize the Kirchhoff index, a spectral measure of network efficiency. This work connects effective resistance, random walks, and network flows.

Paper: Clustering-Based Edge Augmentation for Minimizing the Kirchhoff Index

Optimal Experiment Design

I am developing algorithms to select informative samples for regression models under budget constraints, using prior information to reduce parameter uncertainty and improve estimation accuracy.

Recommendation Systems and Streaming Data

I have investigated recommendation algorithms for sparse, evolving user-item interactions, exploring matrix completion and compressed sensing techniques for streaming data.

Cryptography and Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

I have also explored blockchain security and the BFV fully homomorphic encryption scheme for privacy-preserving machine learning, including techniques for efficient polynomial and modular multiplication.

Teaching

I have served as a teaching assistant for Graduate and Advanced Algorithms, Theory of Machine Learning, Probability and Statistics, Introduction to Data Science, and Introduction to Programming. My responsibilities included lab sessions, office hours, grading, and supporting lecture delivery.
