Prasanth Yalamanchili

PhD Student · Algorithms & Optimization

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of Utah, advised by Prof. Aditya Bhaskara. I develop algorithms for graph optimization, robust network design, and machine learning, using probability, linear algebra, and convex optimization to establish provable guarantees.

My thesis, Edge Augmentations for Robust and Fair Network Design, studies how adding edges can improve network robustness and efficiency. My research also spans structure-preserving feature selection and optimal experiment design.

I earned my BTech in Computer Science from IIT Bhubaneswar in 2019. Before and during my PhD, I worked at Deloitte USI and TaxBit.

Research projects

Algorithms for selecting informative data and building more robust, efficient networks.

  1. 2026–present

    Optimal Experiment Design in Statistical Regression Models

    I am developing algorithms to select informative samples for regression models under budget constraints. This work uses prior information to reduce parameter uncertainty and improve estimation accuracy.

  2. 2025–2026

    Structure-Preserving Feature Selection for High-Dimensional Data

    I developed algorithms to select informative, representative features from high-dimensional datasets under multiple matrix norms. These methods support data summarization and dimensionality reduction, with applications to graph optimization.

    Paper: Graph Augmentation for Min Effective Resistances using Column Subset Selection

  3. 2023–2024

    Optimizing Probabilistic Distances in Social Networks via Link Recommendation

    I designed edge-augmentation algorithms for probabilistic graphs to improve worst-case connectivity. Using tools from percolation theory, I analyzed networks under stochastic failures and proved connectivity guarantees.

    Paper: Optimizing Probabilistic Propagation in Graphs by Adding Edges

  4. 2025

    Optimizing Resistance-Based Flow Metrics in Social Networks

    I developed clustering-based algorithms to minimize the Kirchhoff index, a spectral measure of network efficiency. This work connects edge augmentation with effective resistance, random walks, and network flows.

    Paper: Clustering-Based Edge Augmentation for Minimizing the Kirchhoff Index

  5. 2022–2023

    Recommendation Systems for Streaming Data

    I investigated recommendation algorithms with provable guarantees for sparse, evolving user-item interactions. I explored matrix completion techniques from compressed sensing to address the challenges of streaming data.

  6. Cryptography research · DRDO Delhi & University of Utah

    Blockchain and Fully Homomorphic Encryption

    I explored hashing, digital signatures, zero-knowledge proofs, and pseudorandom generators in the context of Bitcoin and Ethereum security. I also investigated the BFV fully homomorphic encryption scheme for privacy-preserving machine learning, including relinearization and error overheads.

    I explored GPU performance improvements for polynomial and modular multiplication using residue number systems, number-theoretic transforms, and Barrett and Montgomery reductions.
