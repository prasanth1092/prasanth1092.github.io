About Me

I am a PhD student in the Kahlert School of Computing at the University of Utah, advised by Prof. Aditya Bhaskara. My research focuses on combinatorial optimization, graph algorithms, and the foundations of machine learning, using tools from probability, linear algebra, and convex optimization.

Prior to this, I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at IIT Bhubaneswar in 2019 and then worked at Deloitte USI for two years, from 2019 to 2021.

Research

  1. Algebraic Connectivity and Graph Augmentation

    We develop a semidefinite programming relaxation and rounding algorithm with provable approximation guarantees for maximizing algebraic connectivity through graph augmentation.

    Paper: Edge Augmentation for Maximizing Algebraic Connectivity
    Under review at IPCO 2027

  2. Structure-Preserving Feature Selection and Graph Augmentation

    We develop algorithms to select informative, representative features from high-dimensional data under multiple matrix norms. This work connects data summarization and dimensionality reduction with graph augmentation and effective resistance.

    Paper: Graph Augmentation for Minimizing Effective Resistances using Column Subset Selection
    ITCS 2027

  3. Probabilistic Connectivity and Network Robustness

    We study how adding edges can improve worst-case connectivity in probabilistic networks. Using tools from percolation theory, we analyze networks under stochastic failures and design algorithms with connectivity guarantees.

    Paper: Optimizing Probabilistic Propagation in Graphs by Adding Edges
    ITCS 2027 · arXiv

  4. Effective Resistance and Network Efficiency

    We develop clustering-based edge-augmentation algorithms to minimize the Kirchhoff index, a spectral measure of network efficiency. This work connects effective resistance, random walks, and network flows.

    Paper: Clustering-Based Edge Augmentation for Minimizing the Kirchhoff Index
    AISTATS 2026 · PMLR

Other Interests

Recommendation Systems and Streaming Data

We have investigated recommendation algorithms for sparse, evolving user-item interactions, exploring matrix completion and compressed sensing techniques for streaming data.

Cryptography and Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

We have also explored blockchain security and the BFV fully homomorphic encryption scheme for privacy-preserving machine learning, including techniques for efficient polynomial and modular multiplication.
