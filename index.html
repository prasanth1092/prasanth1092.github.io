About

I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of Utah, advised by Prof. Aditya Bhaskara. My thesis is titled Edge Augmentations for Robust and Fair Network Design.

I earned my BTech in Computer Science from IIT Bhubaneswar in 2019 and joined Utah in 2021. My industry experience includes business technology analysis at Deloitte USI and software development on the blockchain team at TaxBit.

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of theoretical computer science, graph optimization, and machine learning. I use probability theory, linear algebra, and convex optimization to design algorithms with provable guarantees.

  • Graph algorithms and network design. Improving network robustness, connectivity, and efficiency through edge augmentation, with a focus on spectral methods, effective resistance, and probabilistic graphs.
  • Data selection and dimensionality reduction. Selecting representative features and informative samples while preserving structure, including column subset selection and optimal experiment design for regression.
  • Learning from sparse and evolving data. Recommendation algorithms, matrix completion, and compressed sensing for streaming user-item interactions.

I have also explored blockchain security and fully homomorphic encryption for privacy-preserving machine learning.

Papers

  1. Graph Augmentation for Min Effective Resistances using Column Subset Selection

    Structure-preserving feature selection under multiple matrix norms, with applications to graph augmentation and effective resistance.

  2. Optimizing Probabilistic Propagation in Graphs by Adding Edges

    Edge-augmentation algorithms for improving worst-case connectivity in probabilistic networks, using percolation theory to analyze stochastic failures.

  3. Clustering-Based Edge Augmentation for Minimizing the Kirchhoff Index

    Clustering-based algorithms for improving network efficiency through the Kirchhoff index, connecting effective resistance, random walks, and network flows.
